Where we work

North-West Nigeria Jigawa, Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Kebbi, Sokoto and Zamfara.

Other zones are not covered because the figures on this site are North-West values. We would rather cover one region honestly than several badly.

Looking for the numbers rather than a conversation? The crops page carries every figure and the working behind it, and sources links to the original report.

Common questions

Asked often enough to answer here.

Why only these eight crops?

They are the crops the NAERLS North-West figures cover well enough to compare fairly. Adding a crop without a reliable regional yield and cost would put a number on the screen that we could not stand behind.

Why is soybean or sesame showing "Unavailable"?

Neither has a comparable North-West July 2024 market price in the survey. Without a price there is no revenue and no return. Their yields and costs are still shown, because those figures are reported.

Are these figures current?

They are from the 2024 wet-season survey, with July 2024 prices. That is the most recent complete regional dataset we have. Prices in particular will have moved since treat the comparison between crops as more reliable than any single figure.

Can my cooperative or extension service use this?

Yes, and please do. If it would be more useful with a printed handout, a Hausa translation, or figures for a different zone, write to us and say which that is how we decide what to build next.

Do you store what I enter?

No. Your answers stay in your browser tab so you can go back and change them, and they are gone when you close it. There is no account, no server and no analytics on your farm details.
