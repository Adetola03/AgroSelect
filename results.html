Before you decide

What the numbers don't tell you.

Timing, risk and water are the things that turn an indicative return into a real one. Read these against your own land.

Important: these are indicative estimates built from the NAERLS 2024 wet-season survey and July 2024 North-West market prices, scaled to the plot size you entered. They are not guaranteed farm profits. Prices move, rains fail, pests arrive, and your soil is your own. Treat this as one input into your decision, alongside your extension officer and your own knowledge of your land.
