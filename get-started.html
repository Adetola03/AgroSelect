Start with what you know about your farm.

Five short questions. They are only used to scale the published figures to your plot and your budget — nothing is sent anywhere or stored on a server.

Check these before you continue

    1 Where is your farm?

    AgroSelect only covers the seven North-West states, because that is the region the survey figures are drawn from.

    Choose the state your farm is in.

    2 How much land will you plant?
    hectares

    Roughly the area you will use this season. One hectare is about two and a half acres, or a plot of 100 m by 100 m.

    Enter your farm size in hectares — it must be more than zero.

    3 What can you spend?
    ₦

    What you can put into this farm before harvest — seed, fertiliser, chemicals and labour. Not your household income.

    Enter the budget you have available for this season.

    4 Which crops are you weighing?

    Pick every crop you are seriously considering — the comparison is only as useful as the options you put in it.

    None selected yet

    Select at least one crop to compare.

    5 How much farming experience do you have?

    This does not change the figures. It only changes which cautions we put in front of you.

    Tell us how much farming experience you have.

    6 Anything you know about your soil?

    Carried through to your results so it sits beside the figures when you decide. Leave it blank if you are not sure.

    You will see estimated cost, expected harvest, revenue and indicative return for each crop you picked — scaled to your plot — from the NAERLS 2024 wet-season survey with July 2024 North-West prices.

    Your answers stay in this browser tab so you can go back and change them. They are not sent to a server and they are cleared when you close the tab.
