  • Indicative return₦1,157,018/ha

  • HarvestAbout 3–4 months

Production & risks

Planting: May–June

Harvest: About 3–4 months

Actual timing can vary with rainfall, irrigation, variety and local growing conditions.

Major risks: Fall armyworm, stem borers, drought or dry spells, and flooding.

How the estimate was calculated

Revenue = 2.50 MT/ha × ₦858/kg ≈ ₦2,147,684/ha. Indicative return = revenue − production cost ≈ ₦1,157,018/ha.

Potential return ₦1,157,018/ha

Evidence

Where the figures come from

Yield and production-cost figures are from the National Agricultural Extension and Research Liaison Services (NAERLS), Agricultural Performance Survey of 2024 Wet Season in Nigeria.

Market-price figures use North-West values from the same report. Where a comparable North-West price was unavailable, AgroSelector shows Unavailable rather than inventing a figure.
